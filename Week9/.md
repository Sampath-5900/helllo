## Reflection on Learning Activities ##

I found knowledge tests and lecture videos helpful to determine the weekly learning. I did use them every week for approximately 30 to 45 minutes. Weak areas were identified during the tests, whereas important concepts like confidentiality, integrity, availability, routing, HTTP, and transport protocols were explained through the videos.
The weekly GitHub journal was helpful as it was asking for actual proof, screenshots, packet analysis and short explanation. Completed journal items for about 1-2 hours a week. It helped me to clarify technical work and relate theory to network related activities.
For me the most helpful revision tool was the Moodle practice activities. I have used them in the tutorial and as a guide to prepare for exams. They were interactive, they provided feedback and they assisted me to apply the concepts and not just definitions. The activities like CIA Triad, Risk Assessment, HTTP Sandwich and routing practice were particularly helpful, since they introduced security concepts to actual network activity.
Overall, the most effective learning activity was the Practice Activities as they provided immediate feedback and identified errors. My advice would be to do more than the minimum amount of activities and repeat any activities that are low (below 4) in score until the topic is understood.

# Conclusion #

Week 09 reinforced previous weeks and prepared for exam. Activities reinforced security concepts, risks of attacks, security measures, IP addressing, routing, and protocol encapsulations. The evidence of participation and completion is shown in the screenshots included in this report.
